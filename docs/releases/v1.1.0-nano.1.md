**First test build of Rabbit Ears for the Anbernic RG Nano (DrUm78 FunKey-OS).**

A port of [Rabbit Ears 1.1](https://github.com/snyderman3000/rabbitears) for the Miyoo Mini: live channels with commercial breaks, the channel guide, DVR mode and vhsify all come across.

New for the Nano:
- Every screen redrawn for the 240×240 panel.
- Its own video player, `tvplay` (FFmpeg + ALSA, inside the OPK), since DrUm78's OS doesn't ship one. Sound goes through the OS's normal audio route, so volume, the speaker and USB-C audio work as usual.
- SETUP → PICTURE: 4:3 (letterboxed) or ZOOM (fills the square screen).
- The power button opens a power menu: resume, turn the TV off, or shut down.

**Install:** copy `Applications` and `Media` from the zip to the root of the SD card, put MP4s (ideally converted with vhsify) in `Media/TV`, and open Rabbit Ears from Applications.

This build hasn't been on a physical RG Nano yet. If anything misbehaves, please open an issue and attach `FunKey/RabbitEars/rabbitears.log` and `stderr.log` from the SD card.
